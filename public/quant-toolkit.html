The Systematic Research Flow

1. Raw Data

Polymarket trade feeds, 1m Binance candles, FRED macro releases.

Why: We start with raw objective reality, no bias.

2. Transformation

Cleaning, resampling to hourly, calculating log returns or prob deltas.

Why: Stationary data avoids "fake" correlations.

3. Feature

The "Signal". e.g. 21-day rolling Z-score of sentiment velocity.

Why: Isolates the specific pattern we bet on.

4. Testing

Walk-forward OOS, purged K-fold, and transaction cost modeling.

Why: Prove it survives reality before losing capital.

Example 1: "Geopolitical Fear Lead" (Polymarket)

Data: Fetch raw outcomePrice history for "Israel-Iran ceasefire" on Polymarket.
Transformation: Calculate 24h change in probability. prob_delta = p[t] - p[t-24h].
Feature (The Signal): If prob_delta < -0.15 (Fear Spike), create a Signal = 1 (Buy Gold).
Testing & Trading: Backtest: "If Fear Spike, buy Gold at next Open and hold for 48h."
Apply 5bps fees. If Sharpe > 1.2 and Win Rate > 55% in Out-of-Sample, we trade.

Example 2: "Recession Momentum" (Macro Release)

Data: Monthly Unemployment Rate (FRED: UNRATE).
Problem: Raw unemployment is highly non-stationary (it has long trends).
Transformation (Ensuring Stationarity): Calculate the 12-month Change in the rate.
unrate_diff = UNRATE[t] - UNRATE[t-12].
This removes the level bias and focuses on the *acceleration* of job loss.
Feature (The Signal): Z-score the difference over a 5-year rolling window.
Signal = (unrate_diff - Mean) / StdDev.
If Signal > 2.0, it indicates a statistically significant labor market breakdown.
Testing & Trading: Backtest: "If Unemployment Z > 2.0, Rotate 100% Equity into 10-Year Treasuries (TLT)."
Why: We use stationarity to ensure we aren't fooled by "normal" unemployment levels, but instead react to the *regime shift* from growth to recession.
